Questions about Horned Giant

What scale should I print Horned Giant at?

The mini comes scaled to ~200mm (D&D Huge-class). For 32mm D&D 5e / Pathfinder tabletop scale, set ~20-27% scale in your slicer. For collectible display, 60-75%.

What's the mesh quality of Horned Giant?

The mini has 600k triangles — clean enough for FDM at medium-large scales, detailed enough for resin printing at small tabletop scales.

Does Horned Giant print well on FDM?

Yes. Recommended FDM settings: 0.12-0.20mm layer height, 10-15% infill, tree supports from the base. Minimal supports needed.

Is Horned Giant supported for resin printing?

Yes. Recommended resin settings: 0.05mm layer, 1.8-3s exposure, light-medium supports from the base. The mesh is watertight and oriented for printing. Hollow in your slicer to save resin.

Any specific print warnings for Horned Giant?

The model has 65 thin-wall regions flagged by our pre-print scan (cloaks, capes, weapon edges). Print at the recommended scale or larger to avoid breakage. The full thin-wall report is included with the STL.

Can I sell physical prints of Horned Giant?

Yes — the license allows you to print and sell the physical figure (conventions, online, custom commissions). What you can NOT do is redistribute or resell the digital STL file itself.
