About This Sculpt

Planted like a signpost at the edge of ruined highlands, this spearman grips his weapon vertically, butt to the ground, left fist clenched at his side. The leaf-bladed spearhead sits above a wrapped shaft, and his kit reads pure working soldier: hooded gorget, cross-strapped leather cuirass, a single pauldron, belt pouches, and a tunic hem torn from the road. Use him as a picket the party must talk past before reaching the mercenary camp — one failed Persuasion check and a Guard statblock earns its keep, with Thug stats if the conversation sours. The spear shaft is the print consideration: it is thin but runs base-to-blade in one straight line, so print him upright with the shaft vertical and it becomes self-reinforcing; resin gives the cleanest spearhead edge.

Questions about Mercenary Spearman

What scale should I print Mercenary Spearman at?

The mini comes scaled to ~120mm (D&D Medium-class). For 32mm D&D 5e / Pathfinder tabletop scale, set ~20-27% scale in your slicer. For collectible display, 60-75%.

What's the mesh quality of Mercenary Spearman?

The mini has 260k triangles — clean enough for FDM at medium-large scales, detailed enough for resin printing at small tabletop scales.

Does Mercenary Spearman print well on FDM?

Yes. Recommended FDM settings: 0.12-0.20mm layer height, 10-15% infill, tree supports from the base. Supports are recommended on cloaks, hoods, capes and trailing fabric.

Is Mercenary Spearman supported for resin printing?

Yes. Recommended resin settings: 0.05mm layer, 1.8-3s exposure, light-medium supports from the base. The mesh is watertight and oriented for printing. Hollow in your slicer to save resin.

Any specific print warnings for Mercenary Spearman?

The model has 24 thin-wall regions flagged by our pre-print scan (cloaks, capes, weapon edges). Print at the recommended scale or larger to avoid breakage. The full thin-wall report is included with the STL.

Can I sell physical prints of Mercenary Spearman?

Yes — the license allows you to print and sell the physical figure (conventions, online, custom commissions). What you can NOT do is redistribute or resell the digital STL file itself.
